ERA Holobox Frame

Renders the live ERA/RINF graph as its own bubble-and-edge view — the same style ERA Graph Explorer draws — on all four faces of a pyramid hologram display. Click a bubble, or Expand a related resource in the dock, to open it as the next bubble across all faces.

Per-face rotation follows the common 4-way pyramid "fan" fold, but the exact angles depend on how your physical reflector is cut and which way it folds — recalibrate the four values above against your unit rather than trusting the defaults. Each face redraws itself to fit whatever size it's given, so the four small faces and the single large "Flat" preview both stay legible without separate tuning.
