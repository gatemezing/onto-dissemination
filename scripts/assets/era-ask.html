Ask the ERA Graph

Ask about railway infrastructure, organisations and EU rail law in plain language — type it or speak it — and this tool turns the question into a live SPARQL query across three ERA endpoints: rinf-plus (infrastructure), OCR-KG (organisations) and era-lex (EU rail legislation). It recognises a fixed set of question shapes by keyword matching, in English, French, German, Spanish or Italian — not a general language model — so an unrecognised question gets a nudge toward the sample questions below rather than a guess.

Every answer is a live SPARQL query run from your browser — nothing is sent anywhere else, and the exact
       query is always one click away under “Show the SPARQL query”. Voice input uses your browser's
       own speech recognition (Chrome/Edge); it is not supported in every browser. This is a demo of a small,
       fixed vocabulary — tunnels, freight terminals, stations, border points, railway lines, high-speed line
       kilometres, infrastructure managers, railway undertakings, and EU rail legislation by topic — not a
       general question-answering system.
