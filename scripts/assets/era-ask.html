Ask the ERA Graph

Ask about railway infrastructure, organisations and EU rail law in plain language — type it or speak it — and this tool turns the question into a live SPARQL query across three ERA endpoints: rinf-plus (infrastructure), OCR-KG (organisations) and era-lex (EU rail legislation). A small open-source language model, Qwen2.5, runs in your browser and reads the question into one of a fixed set of question shapes; each shape has a SPARQL query written and checked by hand, so the model never writes a query itself. Without a WebGPU browser, or until you load the model, keyword matching in English, French, German, Spanish and Italian does the same job.

Every answer is a live SPARQL query run from your browser — nothing is sent anywhere else, and the exact
       query is always one click away under “Show the SPARQL query”. The language model is downloaded
       once from Hugging Face (about 1 GB, then cached by your browser) and runs on your own GPU through
       WebLLM; your questions never leave the page. It only fills in
       a typed form — which question shape, which country, which topic — constrained to a JSON schema, and the
       form it filled is shown under the SPARQL. Voice input uses your browser's
       own speech recognition (Chrome/Edge); it is not supported in every browser. This is a demo of a small,
       fixed vocabulary — tunnels and tunnel length, freight terminals, stations, border points, operational
       points, sections of line, railway lines, high-speed line kilometres, infrastructure managers, railway
       undertakings, an organisation looked up by its code or its name, and EU rail legislation by topic — not
       a general question-answering system.
