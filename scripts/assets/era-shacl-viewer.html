ERA SHACL Shape Viewer

See what a SHACL shapes graph actually asks of the data: every node shape as a card listing its property constraints — path, value type, cardinality, severity, and the SHACL-SPARQL rules that check the same property — with lines to the shapes its values must conform to (left: shapes that point here; right: shapes this one points to). Click any shape to follow it. Starts with ERA's own shapes, read live from the ERA ontology repository; drop in any Turtle, N-Triples or TriG file to view your own.

Live mode sends one CONSTRUCT for the named graph http://data.europa.eu/949/graph/shacl to graph.data.era.europa.eu/repositories/ERA-Onto, straight from your browser. A file you open or paste is parsed on this page and never leaves your machine. A property links to another shape through sh:node, or through sh:class when another shape targets that class — including inside sh:or / sh:xone / sh:and lists. SHACL-SPARQL constraints attached to a node shape are listed under the property they name with era:affectedProperty; the rest stay at shape level.
