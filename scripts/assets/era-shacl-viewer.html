ERA SHACL Shape Viewer

See what a SHACL shapes graph actually asks of the data: every node shape as a card listing its property constraints — path, value type, cardinality, severity, and the SHACL-SPARQL rules that check the same property — with lines to the shapes its values must conform to (left: shapes that point here; right: shapes this one points to). Click any shape to follow it. Starts with ERA's production shapes, read live; switch to UAT for the shapes being tested for the next release, or to Compare to see what UAT changes — shapes and property constraints marked new, changed or removed, with the statements and SPARQL queries before and after. OWL gaps sets the UAT release candidate’s shapes against its ontology’s OWL axioms — where data the shapes accept is inconsistent with the axioms or gains types nobody meant, and what OWL 2 reasoners reject in the axioms themselves — and lists the immediate actions to fix, most severe first. You can also drop in any Turtle, N-Triples or TriG file to view your own.

    Click a shape in a finding to open it below; shapes and properties with gaps carry a badge there.
    PROD sends one CONSTRUCT for the named graph http://data.europa.eu/949/graph/shacl to graph.data.era.europa.eu/repositories/ERA-Onto; UAT reads the published shapes file rinf.uat.data.test-era.europa.eu/era-vocabulary/era-shapes. Both go straight from your browser. Compare matches shapes and property shapes by IRI (a property shape whose IRI changed is matched by its path), compares every statement about them, and compares SHACL-SPARQL constraints by query body and message — differences only in a query's PREFIX declarations are ignored. A file you open or paste is parsed on this page and never leaves your machine. A property links to another shape through sh:node, or through sh:class when another shape targets that class — including inside sh:or / sh:xone / sh:and lists. SHACL-SPARQL constraints attached to a node shape are listed under the property they name with era:affectedProperty; the rest stay at shape level.
    OWL gaps sends two CONSTRUCTs to the UAT endpoint graph.uat.data.test-era.europa.eu/repositories/ERA-Onto, for graph/ontology and graph/shacl, and reads the release list from the GitLab API to name the latest candidate. Measured 2026-11-14: UAT’s graph/ontology matches the ontology.ttl of tag v4.0.0-rc.4 triple for triple (blank nodes apart), and graph/shacl is the union of that tag’s RINF, ERATV, EVR, ERADIS and telematics shape files. The checks read the axioms as OWL 2 does: domains and ranges are inherited from super-properties, functional properties pass their restriction to their sub-properties, and the external subclass axioms of OWL-Time, GeoSPARQL, Simple Features, ORG and FOAF are taken into account. Only shapes that can apply are judged — active, and listed by an active node shape. Reasoning beyond told subclass and disjointness axioms is out of scope; the dated HermiT result in the report is a separate measurement.
