Primary Locations in RINF

Pick a country and get a CSV of every TAF/TAP primary location (era:PrimaryLocation) that RINF publishes for it: the location code, name, infrastructure manager, passenger, freight, container-handling and hand-over flags, validity, position, NUTS 3 region, the dataset it comes from and the operational points that point to it. One row per primary-location code; where a code is published more than once, the description in force on the date you choose (today by default) is kept. To narrow the extract, optionally pick one or more of the country’s national lines: you then get only the primary locations referenced (era:primaryLocation) by the operational points on those lines. No RCC parameters are exported; this is the list of the locations themselves. Queries go live from your browser to graph.data.era.europa.eu; nothing else is sent anywhere.
