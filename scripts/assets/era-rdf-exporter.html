ERA RDF Exporter

Paste any ERA / RINF resource URI and export its full RDF graph as RDF/XML — recursed down to real leaf values, with ontology/SHACL noise and sibling-track blowup automatically filtered out. Parameters the ERA ontology marks owl:deprecated are dropped too, so the export carries only what the current specification permits.
