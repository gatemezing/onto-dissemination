01Summary

The CRD graph replicates RNE's Common Reference Data every day: TAF/TAP primary locations (PLC) and subsidiary locations (SLC). It uses only ERA v3.3.4 terms, and every term it uses exists, undeprecated, in the PROD ontology. Its PLC structure is cleaner than RINF's. Each code has one IRI, and every location is named in English, flagged, dated and attributed to an IM, with a valid code pattern throughout. Against ERA's own shapes, CRD conforms better than RINF: under PROD only two vocabulary-alignment constraints fire.

What the comparison with RINF shows

  • Coverage. 45,733 PLCs are in both sources. 13,422 are in CRD only (GB 7,312, FR 962, PT 825, PL 685, RS 682, DE 550, …); 4,439 are in RINF only (DE 1,935, CH 922, IT 362, PL 271, NO 269, DK 221, EU 91, …).
  • 4,413 PLCs that RINF operational points reference are absent from CRD. Most belong to DB InfraGO (1,934) and the Swiss graphs 0085 + 3915 (905).
  • RINF is already partly CRD-derived. 9,166 of DB InfraGO's 11,104 RINF PLs and all 780 of ProRail's carry this pipeline's IRI fingerprints, so agreement there is reported separately.
  • Validity start dates disagree on 59 % of the independent overlap. The most likely reason is that the two sources date different events.
  • Subsidiary locations do not overlap. RINF's 3,899 SLs are Bane NOR tracks; CRD's 75,917 are the RNE SLC catalogue. Not one (PLC, SLC) pair is shared.

03Conformance with the ERA ontology and shapes

3.1 Ontology

  • Vocabulary. All 8 classes and 29 properties the data uses are declared in ERA PROD v3.3.4, none deprecated, with the expected domains and ranges. The deprecated era:tafTAPCode is not used.
  • CRD's copy of the ontology is graph-isomorphic to PROD's once line endings are normalised. 296 literals use \r\n where PROD uses \n.
  • ParameterApplicability is modelled correctly. It is a subclass of era:TemporalFeature, so time:hasBeginning directly on it is right. None of the 54,572 applicabilities contradicts its flag.
  • Re-declared terms (F6). Both data graphs carry full ERA descriptions of 37 terms, including owl:Ontology <http://data.europa.eu/949/>.

Bane NOR does model km posts differently, but the shapes do not check it. All 732 posts hang on one nationwide LPS (_lps02, no era:lineId) as an integer grid from km −2 to 729, and none of its 35 line LPSs carries a post. 3.4 Notes for ERA's shape maintainers

These problems in ERA's own shapes came to light while running them. They have nothing to do with CRD.

  • PROD LangStringEnRequired and LangStringEnRequiredIfPresent are invalid SPARQL once $PATH is substituted, because SELECT $this $PATH projects an IRI. UAT already reads SELECT $this.
  • The ontology declares both geo: and geosparql: for one namespace. rdflib-based validators bind only one, so the other stops resolving.
  • Constraints use VALUES, MINUS and nested SELECT, which SHACL 1.0's pre-binding rules forbid; pyshacl rejects them by default.
  • 17 UAT constraints use SERVICE and cannot run on a local copy.
  • Severity on sh:SPARQLConstraint nodes is ignored. The intended Warnings report as Violations.
  • era:inNUTS3 points at a scheme with no members (F10).

05Findings in detail

F1MAJORAllocation companies typed as infrastructure managers

Every era:allocationCompany points at …/organisationRole/{code}_IM, which has era:hasOrganisationRole organisation-roles/IM. In OCR-KG, 11 of the 34 companies hold no IM role, and they cover 30,059 SLs: UIC (3011, 17,559 SLs, role IRO), Rail Cargo Austria, DB Cargo, ČD Cargo, SBB Cargo, Rail Cargo Hungaria, UIRR, CFL Cargo, Gysev Cargo, DB Cargo Hungária and Rail&Sea. As published, the graph says UIC is an infrastructure manager.

Point allocationCompany at an era:OrganisationRole carrying the role the company actually holds in OCR-KG, and keep the IM role for era:infrastructureManager.

F2MAJORGreat Britain at null island

All 7,312 GB PLs, plus one each in DE, FR and CZ, have geo:asWKT "POINT(0 0)" and wgs84:lat "0.0". A map draws them in the Gulf of Guinea, and nearest-neighbour and bounding-box queries are skewed.

Omit netReference/hasGeometry when CRD has no coordinates. UAT then raises its intended geometry warning instead of accepting a fake point.

F3MAJORSubsidiary-location types from UAT

19,990 SLs use type codes 01 (19,051), 15 (598), 03 (327), 10 (11), 27 (2) and 02 (1). These exist in the UAT scheme (50 codes) but not in PROD's (37). CRD's own graph/era/skos is PROD's scheme plus 293 UAT schemes, so the data fails CRD's own vocabulary too.

Pin one release, PROD or UAT, for both vocabulary and validation, and record the choice in the dataset metadata.

F4MAJORSL properties outside the UAT closed shape no issue under PROD

UAT closes SubsidiaryLocationShape, so era:inCountry (75,917), era:infrastructureManager (75,838) and era:operatingLanguage (75,913) each produce a sh:ClosedConstraintComponent violation on every SL.

The properties are useful, so either ERA adds them to the closed shape, or CRD derives them from the linked PL and stops asserting them on the SL. Raise this with ERA before UAT is promoted.

F5MAJORDanish codes identify different places RINF side · verified on samples

For 289 of the 297 shared DK codes, the CRD and RINF positions are more than 10 km apart. In 256 cases, the CRD position of one code exactly equals the RINF position of another. For example, RINF's OP uopid DK00010 "Høje Taastrup" sits at POINT(12.269823 55.648536), which is CRD's DK00005 Høje Taastrup; CRD's DK00010 is Borup, 25 km away. RINF's Danish PLs carry no names, so name matching cannot catch the error.

Report this to Banedanmark and ERA. Until it is fixed, do not join Danish data on code.

F6–F9MINORModelling and hygiene

F6. The ontology is re-declared inside the data graphs; keep the schema in graph/era/ontology only. F7. era:NetBasicReference carries untyped wgs84:lat/lon next to a typed geo:wktLiteral; drop the WGS84 triples. F8. None appears in IRIs, apparently a serialised Python None. The same IRIs already exist in RINF's DE/NL graphs, so rename them only together with ERA. F9. 236 PL and 377 SL names contain runs of whitespace; normalise them.

F10MINORNUTS IRIs partly on the ERA side

The ontology declares era:inNUTS3's scheme as https://publications.europa.eu/resource/authority/nuts, but no concept in ERA-Onto is skos:inScheme it. ERA's graph/nuts uses http://data.europa.eu/nuts/code/{code}, which CRD follows, while RINF's Austrian publishers use the Publications Office IRIs. A join on IRI therefore returns nothing, and InNUTS3SKOS fires for every value in either form.

ERA should settle on one IRI form, and CRD should follow it.

F11SUGGESTIONGovernance and provenance

IRIs. All CRD resources are minted under ERA's namespace by a third party. For PLs this is useful, because 38,105 codes share the IRI with RINF, but it also means CRD triples merge silently with ERA's. Agree the pattern with ERA, or mint under a CRD namespace and link with owl:sameAs. Provenance. Add a publisher, a licence, prov:wasDerivedFrom (RNE CRD) and the snapshot timestamp. SKOS. Load PROD as it is, or label the graph as UAT. Validity. CRD appears to drop retired codes. Keeping them with a closed validity window would keep RINF's references to them resolvable.

06Method, reproducibility and limits

The queries, scripts and output files are all in crd-kg/. Each field is extracted by its own query, which keeps RINF under its 120 s limit, and the extracts are joined locally on era:primaryLocationCode.

python3 -I scripts/fetch.py                # ~1 min, both endpoints → data/raw/ (git-ignored)
python3 -I scripts/compare.py 2026-11-03   # joins on the PLC → stats/*.csv, stats/summary.json
python3 -I scripts/sparql.py crd queries/crd-checks.rq
python  -I scripts/validate.py SAMPLE.ttl SHAPES.ttl OUT.csv REF1.ttl REF2.ttl …

Limits.

  • Join key. Where a code means different things on each side (F5), agreement figures say nothing for that country.
  • RINF duplicates. RINF was not deduplicated for yearly republishing, and a field counts as agreeing when it matches any RINF variant. This flatters RINF slightly.
  • SHACL scope. SHACL ran on a sample; every constraint that fired was then counted exactly over the full CRD dataset. The 17 SERVICE constraints were skipped, and pyshacl's pre-binding checks were relaxed so that ERA's queries run as ERA runs them.
  • Roles. F1 relies on OCR-KG's role list as of 3 November 2026.
