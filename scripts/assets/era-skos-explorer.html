ERA SKOS Explorer

Browses the SKOS concept schemes that govern ERA/RINF's controlled-vocabulary properties — things like operational point type, rail inclination or current type, each bound to a scheme via era:inSkosConceptScheme. Pick a scheme to see its concepts (notation, label, definition), then check it against what the data actually uses: values present in the data but not declared as scheme members are exactly the kind of gap a malformed IRI or a mis-typed value creates — silently, since nothing in the endpoint validates a skos:ConceptScheme the way it validates a skos:Concept. Export to CSV or Excel. Queries graph.data.era.europa.eu live from your browser; nothing else is sent anywhere.

Discovers schemes two ways — directly (?scheme a skos:ConceptScheme) and via every property that
       declares one (?property era:inSkosConceptScheme ?scheme) — since a scheme need not be reachable
       by both. Concept membership is checked three ways too (skos:inScheme,
       skos:hasTopConcept, skos:topConceptOf), since ERA's datasets are not consistent
       about which one they publish. Labels prefer English but accept an untagged literal, the same gap found and
       fixed in the RCC Parameters and Route Book tools — some concepts here carry no language tag at all.

    
The "URI-pattern outlier" check flags concepts whose URI doesn't share the scheme's own dominant path prefix
       — the same shape of bug the reusability answers page found by
       hand for Operational Point Types: 8 concepts published at …/op-types/OperationalPointTypes/{n}
       instead of the scheme's own …/op-types/{notation}, with no skos:notation and
       Title-Case labels carrying typos. This tool surfaces that class of problem for every scheme with a bound
       property, not just that one.
