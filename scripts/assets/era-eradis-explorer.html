Description/title

ERADIS-KG does not record the subsystem itself. It is derived from the TSIs the declaration cites, looked up in the era-lex repository: a TSI's title names its subsystem, and an amending act takes the subsystem of the act it amends (eli:changes). The result is mapped onto the subsystems of Directive (EU) 2016/797 Annex II, so the noise, wagon and locomotive TSIs all count as rolling stock. The PRM and tunnel-safety TSIs, and acts amending several subsystems at once (e.g. 2019/776), name none; search those with “To TSIs”.
