What is real and what is not. Classes, properties, constraints and cardinalities come from ERA's
      UAT shapes (rinf-appGuide/shapes.ttl). Coded values are real concepts from the ERA SKOS schemes, countries
      and languages from the EU authority lists, and the infrastructure manager is the country's main IM role as published in RINF.
      Positions follow the real network: sections and points are drawn near operational points sampled live from RINF, then
      shifted by a few tens of metres. Names, identifiers (FRS…, the S marks them synthetic) and
      IRIs are invented. Never mix this data with real RINF data.

    
Engine. RDFGraphGen 1.2.0 (MIT, Jovanovik et al.) runs unmodified in your browser through Pyodide
      0.28.3, with rdflib 7.6.0. The shapes are first stripped of what RDFGraphGen cannot read or does not use
      (sh:not, SHACL-SPARQL); see the How it works tab. The Pyodide loader is integrity-checked; the Python
      packages come from PyPI at pinned versions.
