RCC Parameters at Primary Locations

Pick a country and one of its national lines. The tool finds the line's operational points that are linked to at least one TAF/TAP primary location (era:primaryLocation) and returns a CSV of every RINF parameter flagged era:usedInRCCCalculations at those points (index 1.2). That covers the point itself, its running tracks and sidings, their contact-line, ETCS, train-detection and hot-axle-box sub-objects, and their platform edges and tunnels. Each place keeps the validity window in force on the date you choose (today by default), and retired parameters are excluded. Queries go live from your browser to graph.data.era.europa.eu; nothing else is sent anywhere.

    Which operational points. A point qualifies when it is on the chosen line and carries at least one era:primaryLocation. A point is on the line when it is positioned by a kilometric post on the line's own linear positioning system (LPS), or when a section of the line names it as era:opStart/era:opEnd. Both are needed: French line 830000-1 reaches 327 points one way and 603 the other way. Norway hangs every kilometric post on one nationwide LPS rather than on its lines, so its points are found only through section endpoints, and Croatia publishes no LPS coordinate at all. RINF links points to primary locations only in this direction; the inverse era:isPrimaryLocationOf is not used (measured 8 November 2026).

    Validity: the window in force on the chosen date, per place, never per line. Each point keeps the resource whose validity window is in force on the “Valid on” date (today by default), chosen per era:uopid. Each track and siding is chosen the same way, keyed by era:trackId or era:sidingId within the point, never by the point's newest date: France dates each track by the day it opened, and a per-point rule would drop older tracks that are still current. Simply taking the newest window is wrong once a manager publishes ahead. DB InfraGO's points carry a 2026-01-01 → 2026-12-31 window and a from-2027-01-01 window, and in the 2027 one about 3,150 primary-location links are missing (measured 8 November 2026). A place with no window in force uses its most recent window and is marked “(ended)”. A place whose windows all lie in the future is left out and counted. Resources with no dated validity are always kept, because ten countries publish none.

    The part-whole direction is probed once per run, because Croatia publishes only era:hasPart and the train-detection parameters at points are reachable only that way. Asking for both directions in one query is correct but makes the endpoint unusably slow.

    Values. Concepts are resolved to their English label where one exists. A code with no label (for example a retired GSM-R version) is shown as its IRI, so a non-conformance stays visible. Geometries are given as WKT. Several values for one parameter on one element are joined with “ | ”. A parameter with several RINF numbers is given the number that matches where it was found: 1.2.0 on the point, 1.2.1 on a running track and its parts, 1.2.2 on a siding and its parts. position_km is kilometer + offset/1000; it is correct whether the publisher counts from the preceding kilometric post or, like Norway, from the nearest one.
