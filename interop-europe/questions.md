## Labelled solutions have to be assessed for their reusability in interoperability assessments. 

- Would it make sense for public authorities to look in this context at the reusability of your solution for the implementation of the assessed decision?
- What was your main challenge to implement this solution, and which were the main lessons learnt following the implementation?
- Do you envisage collaboration with other Member States or Union entities, e.g. around future developments of the solutions, and how?
- Is your solution suitable for local, regional, or national public administrations? 
- Does it need central governance, or does it also bring value, if deployed in just some entities?
- How does your solution support the data exchange between borders and EU sovereignty?
- What is the first step to take to reuse your solution?
